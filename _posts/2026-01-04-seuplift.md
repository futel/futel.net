---

Futel has received a 2026 Community Small Grant from the SE Uplift Neighborhood Coalition. This will help us provide a free telephone to offer low-barrier communication tools for patrons of the Sunnyside Shower Project.

[The Sunnyside Shower Project](https://www.sunnysideshowerproject.org) is a volunteer-powered mutual aid project which provides free showers, hygiene supplies, clean clothing, laundry access, and connections to supportive resources in Southeast Portland.
