![](/images/blog/PXL_20250503_232231684.jpg)

New phone at Street Roots! West Burnside at 3rd, north side of the street, in Old Town Chinatown. Our first public phone west of the river! The enclosure was formerly in front of the old Street Roots building.

This phone will feature audio works from Street Roots vendors in their creative writing workshop. We are very enthusiastic about partnering with Street Roots and have been working to make this project happen for a long time! Supported by a Portland Arts Project grant from the Regional Arts and Culture Council and the Arts Access Fund.
